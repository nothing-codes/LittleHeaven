Home is where the carpet is

My flat is not very big, but it is bright, warm and always tidy. I live on the ground floor, and every morning the sun fills the lounge with soft golden light.

The lounge is my favourite room. There is a grey settee in the centre of the room and a mustard-yellow comfy chair next to the table, where I enjoy scrolling through TikTok. A thick beige carpet covers the floor, so the room feels cosy even in winter.

The kitchen is small but smart. It has a grey microwave on the blue counter, a deep steel sink and a shiny new tap that never drips. The cabinets are also blue, keeping the room tidy and stylish.

The bathroom is clean and calm: a glass shower, a mirror cabinet and a plastic laundry basket hidden in the corner.

I love my flat because everything in it has its own place — and its own little story.
